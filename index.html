Counterfeit

A humble and talentless painter wished for greater things. They didn't realise that something else was listening...

A first person painting game about replicating artwork, with a lighthearted occult story. The demo contains a prologue and the first level of the campaign, as well as a solo mode to replicate paintings at your own leisure.
